FAQ

Common questions

What's included in the $39.99 check-up?

A full inspection of your system, controller programming, head and rotor adjustment, answers to your questions, and a no-obligation estimate for any repairs.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. Our local teams are on call for burst pipes and major leaks. Call 1-877-517-4675.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Fully licensed and insured, TCEQ Licensed Irrigator LI#0019350, and BBB Accredited with an A+.

Do I need winterization in Texas?

Yes. A cold snap can freeze and burst pipes, valves, and heads. Our fall winterization blows the water out of the system. It's included in every maintenance plan.

Do you do drainage?

Yes. Pop-up drains, channel drains, catch basins, and more, with free estimates on drain installation and cleaning.

Where are you located?

Our Houston office is at 5635 NW Central Drive, Suite 103, Houston, TX 77092. We also serve Austin and Dallas.
